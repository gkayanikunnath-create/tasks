The data associated with 17 relaxed galaxy groups are provided as separate .dat files under "tasks/environment/data/", with file corresponding to an individual galaxy. Each file contains the total acceleration and the baryonic acceleration data measured in radial bins, along with their quoted acceleration uncertainties. 
Using the provide galaxy group data, test for the validity of the radial acceleration relations (RAR) at galaxy-group scales. Summarize the RAR results and the estimated acceleration scale for the group data, including the associated uncertainty. Compare the inferred acceleration scale and the RAR behavior of this galaxy group data with the previously known RAR results at galaxy scales.
Provide the best fit RAR results and the estimated acceleration scales in a tabular form. Also generate publication-quality plots for:

1. Showing the group data, associated errors, and the fitted radial acceleration relation.
2. Corner plot displaying the 68%, 90% and 99% marginalized credible intervals for the best fit values.

All input and output paths must use absolute paths under "tasks/environment/data/".
