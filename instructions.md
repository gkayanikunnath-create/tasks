The data associated with 17 relaxed galaxy groups are provided as separate .dat files under "tasks/environment/data/", with file corresponding to an individual galaxy. Each file contains the total acceleration and the baryonic acceleration data measured in radial bins, along with their quoted acceleration uncertainties. 
Using the provide galaxy group data, test for the validity of the radial acceleration relations (RAR) at galaxy-group scales. Summarize the RAR results and the estimated acceleration scale for the group data, including the associated uncertainty. Compare the inferred acceleration scale and the RAR behaviour of this galaxy group data with the previously known RAR results at galaxy scales.
Provide the best fit RAR results, estimated acceleration scale, together with a publication-quality plot showing the group data, associated errors, and the best-fit line sowing the radial acceleration relation.
